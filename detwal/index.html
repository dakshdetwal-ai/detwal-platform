Our Service Model

A managed trading experience

The proposed service is designed to centralize trading operations, account monitoring and payout coordination. Availability depends on legal approval, account-provider rules and operational readiness.

Trading operations

Trading activity would be handled centrally under a clearly disclosed and authorized arrangement.

Account monitoring

Customers would be able to review account status and activity through their DETwal dashboard.

Payout coordination

Eligible payout requests would be coordinated under provider rules. Payouts are never guaranteed.
