Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how DETwal handles information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase access, apply for testing, or contact support. Please read it before using the service.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your email address and the name you provide.
  • Profile and support details, including your mobile number and messages you send us.
  • Payment-request details you submit, such as payer UPI ID, transaction reference (UTR), selected plan and amount. We use these to manually match and review payments.
  • Referral, subscription, invoice, notification and service-usage records associated with your account.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed by our hosting and authentication providers to operate and secure the website.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide customer support, process and review payment requests, manage subscriptions and referrals, communicate important service updates, prevent abuse, and maintain our records.

Service providers and sharing

DETwal uses Supabase for authentication and database services and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers process information as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights.

Security and retention

We use access controls and database permissions intended to limit access to account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet recordkeeping obligations, and enforce our terms.

Your choices

You may update available profile details or contact support to request help with account information. Some transaction and security records may need to be retained. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or payment credentials to support.

Children and eligibility

DETwal is not intended for anyone who is not legally eligible to enter into the relevant service and payment agreements in their jurisdiction. A parent or legal guardian must handle any permitted use by a minor where applicable law requires it.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the DETwal Help Desk.

Important: This page is a general privacy notice and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional for the laws and jurisdictions where DETwal operates.
