Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how DETwal handles information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase a TopG Algo licence, apply for testing or contact support.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your email address, account ID and the name you provide.
  • Profile and support details, including your mobile number and messages you send us.
  • Payment-request details you submit, such as payer UPI ID, transaction reference (UTR), selected plan and amount, so we can review and match payments.
  • Plan, licence, terminal activation, subscription, invoice, referral, notification and service-usage records associated with your DETwal account.
  • Basic technical information processed by our hosting, authentication and security providers to operate and protect the website.
  • If a future feature is separately introduced and enabled with notice and consent, limited trading-account metadata such as platform, broker/server name, account login identifier, terminal status and execution diagnostics. We will explain the exact fields and purpose before collecting them.

Information we do not currently request

The current TopG Algo terminal-licence plans do not require your MT5 master password, investor password, broker password, one-time code, UPI PIN or remote-access credentials. Do not send these secrets to DETwal through any channel. We do not currently provide third-party funded-account allocation or managed account operation.

Promotional demo wheel

If you choose to use the TopG Algo promotional demo wheel, we collect the name and age you submit to process the entry and apply the 18+ eligibility check. The page also creates a random browser/device identifier stored in your browser's local storage to enforce one entry per stored device identifier. The entry service receives your IP address from the hosting request and stores only a keyed, one-way hash of it for security and abuse review; we do not store the raw IP address in the promo entry table. The entry record includes the submitted name, age, device hash, IP hash and entry timestamp. Entries are retained only as reasonably necessary for promotion administration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Clearing browser storage or using another browser/device may affect device recognition; IP addresses are not a perfect device identifier and shared networks may be used by multiple people. The demo wheel's outcome is fixed and disclosed on the page; it is not a prize draw and does not award a prize.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure DETwal accounts, activate and administer software licences, enforce terminal limits, provide customer support, review payment requests, manage subscriptions and referrals, communicate service updates, prevent abuse and maintain business records.

Service providers and sharing

DETwal uses Supabase for authentication and database services and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers process information as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or our legal rights. If we add a new service provider that processes personal or trading-account data, we will update this policy as appropriate.

Security and retention

We use access controls and database permissions intended to limit access to account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet recordkeeping obligations and enforce our terms. Retention periods may differ for payment, security and legal records.

Your choices and requests

You may update available profile details or contact support to request help with personal information. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some transaction, security and legal records may need to be retained. You can stop using the service and request account closure through the Help Desk, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Children and eligibility

DETwal services are not intended for people who are not legally eligible to enter into the relevant service and payment agreements in their jurisdiction. Where applicable law requires a parent or legal guardian, that person must handle the permitted account and payment arrangements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, data practices or legal requirements change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. If we introduce trading-account connectivity or managed hosting, we will provide a specific explanation of the data collected, access controls, retention and any relevant third-party processing before enabling that feature.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the DETwal Help Desk.
