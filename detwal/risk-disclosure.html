Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Trading involves substantial risk. You can lose some or all of the money in your trading account. Automated execution does not make trading safe or guarantee profits.

Market, leverage and account risk

Gold, foreign exchange, CFDs and other leveraged products can move rapidly. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Prices can gap, spreads can widen, liquidity can fall, and orders may be rejected, delayed or filled at prices different from those requested. You may lose money quickly. A broker, evaluation provider or funded-trading programme may impose additional restrictions, drawdown limits, fees, eligibility requirements or payout conditions.

Automated-system and connectivity risk

Automated software can experience connectivity failures, platform outages, broker-side changes, data errors, configuration mistakes, slippage, execution delays, software defects and unexpected market conditions. Stops may not execute at the requested price, and a system may behave differently across brokers, symbols, account types and market sessions. A local computer, VPS, network or third-party service can fail while a position is open.

Terminal licences and third-party account rules

A DETwal terminal licence only authorizes use of the software within the purchased terminal limit. It does not provide a funded account, trading capital, a payout entitlement, broker services or permission to use an EA or copier on a particular account. You must check the current rules of your broker and any evaluation or funded-trading programme before using automated trading, copying, remote hosting or VPS services. Provider rules can change and a breach may result in account restrictions, termination or denied payouts.

Do not share account credentials or permit third-party account access unless the account provider expressly allows it. DETwal does not currently allocate or operate third-party funded accounts.

Backtests and historical performance

Backtested, simulated, hypothetical or historical results are not live performance and may not reflect real execution costs, slippage, liquidity, spreads or changing market conditions. Past performance, win rates, accuracy figures and winning streaks do not predict future results. No performance figure is a promise.

No guarantee and no personal advice

DETwal and TopG Algo do not guarantee profits, capital protection, a particular win rate, funded status, payouts or uninterrupted operation. Website content and software are not personalized investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any financial instrument. You make your own trading decisions and remain responsible for your account.

Use appropriate safeguards

  • Understand the product and provider rules before enabling automated execution.
  • Test in a demo environment before considering live use.
  • Use only money you can afford to lose and consider independent professional advice.
  • Set conservative position sizes and risk limits; monitor execution and connectivity.
  • Keep your platform and account credentials private and secure.
  • Stop the software if its behaviour differs from the documented strategy or your risk settings.

Jurisdiction and eligibility

Trading products and services may not be available or appropriate in every country. You are responsible for checking local laws, regulatory requirements, tax treatment and provider eligibility before using them.

By using a trading-related product, you acknowledge that you understand these risks and accept responsibility for your own trading decisions.
