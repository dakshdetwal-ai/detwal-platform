Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Trading involves substantial risk. You may lose some or all trading capital. No trading strategy or protection feature can guarantee that an account will survive or receive a payout.

Market and execution risk

Prices can gap, spreads can widen, liquidity can disappear and orders can be rejected, delayed or filled at worse prices. A stop-loss is not a guarantee of the exact exit price. Sudden candles and news releases can cause losses beyond the intended risk.

News-event protection limits

The Premium tier includes additional controls around scheduled high-impact news. These controls cannot prevent every sudden price move, stop-loss trigger or account breach. Pro would not include this additional feature. Neither tier can guarantee protection from breaches.

Provider and payout risk

Account providers set their own rules, eligibility, drawdown limits, payout conditions and prohibited practices. They may change rules, restrict accounts or deny payouts. Any future service must be expressly permitted by the provider; DETwal cannot override provider decisions.

Automation and operational risk

Software faults, connectivity failures, VPS outages, latency, broker changes, data errors and human mistakes can affect execution. Historical or backtested performance does not predict future results.

No guarantees or personalized advice

DETwal does not guarantee profits, account survival, funding, payout or uninterrupted operation. Website content is not personalized investment, financial, legal or tax advice.

Before any service launch

Do not share UPI PINs, OTPs, banking passwords or trading passwords. Review the customer agreement, account eligibility, provider restrictions and risk controls before using the service.
