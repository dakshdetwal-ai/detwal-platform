Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Trading involves substantial risk. You can lose some or all of the money in your trading account. Automated execution does not make trading safe or guarantee profits.

Market and leverage risk

Gold, foreign exchange, CFDs and other leveraged products can move rapidly. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Prices can gap, spreads can widen, liquidity can fall, and orders may be rejected, delayed or filled at prices different from those requested. You may lose money quickly.

Automated-system risk

Automated software can experience connectivity failures, platform outages, broker-side changes, data errors, configuration mistakes, slippage, execution delays, software defects and unexpected market conditions. Stops may not execute at the requested price, and a system may behave differently across brokers, symbols, account types and market sessions.

Backtests and historical performance

Backtested, simulated, hypothetical or historical results are not live performance and may not reflect real execution costs, slippage, liquidity, spreads or changing market conditions. Past performance, win rates, accuracy figures and winning streaks do not predict future results. No performance figure is a promise.

No guarantee and no personal advice

DETwal and TopG Algo do not guarantee profits, capital protection, a particular win rate or uninterrupted operation. Website content and software are not personalized investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any financial instrument. You make your own trading decisions and remain responsible for your account.

Use appropriate safeguards

  • Understand the product and broker rules before enabling automated execution.
  • Test in a demo environment before considering live use.
  • Use only money you can afford to lose and consider independent professional advice.
  • Set conservative position sizes and risk limits; monitor execution and connectivity.
  • Check your broker's, exchange's and prop firm's rules. Automated trading may be restricted or prohibited by some providers.

Jurisdiction and eligibility

Trading products and services may not be available or appropriate in every country. You are responsible for checking local laws, regulatory requirements, tax treatment and provider eligibility before using them.

By using a trading-related product, you acknowledge that you understand these risks and accept responsibility for your own trading decisions.
